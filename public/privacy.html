Privacy

Your data on PromptCollab

The short version: PromptCollab stores only what it needs to work, keeps it on the server it runs on, never sells or shares it, and has no ads or trackers. This copy of PromptCollab is run by the person running this server; if you have questions, ask them.

What's stored

  • Your account: username, display name, color, profile picture (if you add one), and when you joined. Your password and recovery codes are stored only as one-way scrambled hashes (scrypt), so nobody can read them, including whoever runs the server.
  • Rooms: each room's name, the shared prompt and system prompt, the conversation (what was sent and the AI's replies), who wrote and sent each message, which model was used, and token counts.
  • Who has access: each room's host, members, people who joined through a link, access requests, and people the host removed.
  • Contacts: who you're connected to, and pending requests.
  • API keys: encrypted (AES-256-GCM). They're never shown again after you save them; only their last 4 characters are.
  • Free message counts: how many free messages each person used today, if the site offers them. Reset daily.
  • Sign-in sessions: a record that your browser is signed in, for up to 30 days. Only a scrambled copy of the session token is stored.

Everything is saved as files on the server running PromptCollab. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Who can see it

  • People in a room see its prompt, conversation, and the names and pictures of others in it.
  • A room's host also sees who has access and who asked to join.
  • Anyone signed in can find you by your exact username (to add you as a contact or to a room), and sees your display name and picture.
  • Nobody sees your API keys, password or recovery codes.
  • Whoever runs the server can technically read the files, except passwords and codes (hashed) and keys (encrypted).

What's sent to AI companies

Only when someone presses Send or Regenerate: the prompt, the room's system prompt, and the conversation so far go to the company behind the chosen model (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or OpenRouter), using the API key of the person who pressed it. What they do with it is covered by that company's own privacy policy. The free demo model sends nothing anywhere.

Free models without a key: if this site offers free messages, they're sent to OpenRouter using the site owner's account, so OpenRouter sees them as coming from that account. Some free models run on providers that may use prompts to train their AI, depending on that account's OpenRouter privacy settings. Don't put anything private into a free model. With your own key, your own OpenRouter settings apply instead. To count your daily free messages, the server stores how many you've used today; that count is reset every day.

Cookies and tracking

There is one cookie that keeps you signed in. If this site uses an access code, a second cookie remembers that you entered it, so you aren't asked again. There are no ads, analytics or trackers, and pages load nothing from other websites (even the fonts are served by this server). Your browser also remembers a few preferences locally, like dark mode and whether you've seen the room walkthrough; those never leave your device.

Your choices

  • Download your data or delete your account from your account page. Deleting removes your account, keys, picture and contacts, and hands rooms you host to the first person you added (or deletes them).
  • Remove an API key any time on the API keys page.
  • Messages you sent stay in a room's conversation for the people still in it, unless the room is cleared or deleted.
