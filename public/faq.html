Questions and answers

Everything people usually ask about PromptCollab. Can't find yours? Email me.

The basics

What is PromptCollab?

A shared editor for AI prompts. Your team writes one prompt together in real time, like a Google Doc, then sends it to a model. The answer streams in for everyone in the room at once, and the conversation stays in the room.

Do I need an account?

Yes, to start or join a room, so people in a room can see who wrote what. Signing up only asks for a username, a display name and a password. No email needed.

While PromptCollab is in private preview, you also need an access code from the owner. Everything on the home, About and FAQ pages is open to anyone.

Is it free?

PromptCollab itself is free. Paid AI models (like GPT, Claude or Gemini) are billed by the company that runs them, to the API key of whoever presses Send.

You can also use OpenRouter's free models, which cost nothing, and a built-in demo model that doesn't send anything anywhere.

Which AI models can I use?

Models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Google with your own key for each, and hundreds more through OpenRouter, including free ones. The model menu only shows models you can actually use.

Keys and cost

Who pays when someone presses Send?

The person who presses Send. Their own API key is used for that message, and nobody else's. If you don't have a key for a model, you can't send to it, but you can still edit the prompt and read every answer.

Do my teammates need their own keys?

Only to send. Anyone in the room can write the prompt and read the replies. If one person has a key and the others don't, that person presses Send when the team is ready. Each key only ever pays for its owner's messages.

How do the free messages work?

When the site owner turns them on, everyone gets 5 free messages a day on OpenRouter's free models, without a key. They reset every day at midnight UTC (the site shows when, in your time zone). On a busy day the free models can be slow or full; adding your own free OpenRouter key gets around that.

Is my API key safe?

Keys are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before they're saved, never shown again after you add them (only the last 4 characters), and never sent to other people's browsers. The site only accepts keys over a secure connection. You can remove a key at any time.

Tip: set a monthly spending limit on your key in the provider's dashboard, so a mistake can never cost much.

Rooms

How do I invite people to a room?

Click Share in the room. You can add teammates by username, or open the room to anyone who has the link. New rooms start restricted: only people you add can get in, and others can ask to join.

Can everyone in a room edit?

Yes. Everyone who can get into a room can edit the prompt and send it. The host (whoever started the room) decides who gets in, and can remove people.

Does it handle code and math?

Yes. Replies show code blocks with a Copy button, tables, and equations written in LaTeX. You can also export a whole conversation as a Markdown file.

Privacy

What gets stored about me?

Only what the app needs: your account, the rooms you're in and their conversations, and your encrypted keys. No ads, analytics or trackers. The privacy page lists everything.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. On your account page you can download all your data or delete your account. Deleting removes your account, keys, picture and contacts.

Are free models private?

Not always. Some free models are run by providers that may use prompts to train their AI. Don't put anything private or confidential into a free model.
